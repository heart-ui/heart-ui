Add the `drawer` component: a panel on Reka `Dialog` that slides in from the `start`, `end` (default) or `bottom` edge, following the text direction, with `DrawerTrigger`, `DrawerContent`, `DrawerHeader`, `DrawerTitle`, `DrawerDescription`, `DrawerFooter` and `DrawerClose` parts. `size` (`sm`, `md`, `lg`) caps a side drawer's width or a bottom drawer's height, and `dismissible`, `showClose` and `closeLabel` work as on `dialog` (P2-15).
