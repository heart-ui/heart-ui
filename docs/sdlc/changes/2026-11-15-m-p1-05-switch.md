## Decisions

| Decision | Ruled out | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `Switch.vue` copies `checkbox`: a `label` wrapping Reka `SwitchRoot` and the slot; `class` on the label, other attributes on the control; an `h-lh` span centres the track on the first line; a disabled switch mutes its text and dims the track | — | The reference row `checkbox`. |
| An own hidden native checkbox for forms, under the track, as in `checkbox` and `radio-group` | Reka's form input | Reka's input sits at a far corner, keeps focus on validation (keys then change it behind `v-model`), the problems fixed in P1-03/P1-04. |
| New lib item `form-control` (`registry/lib/form-control`) with `handFocus` and `fireFormEvents`, used by `checkbox`, `radio-group` and `switch` | a third copy of the code | The ticket from P1-03/P1-04: three controls share it now. |
| Track `h-5 w-9` (md), thumb `size-4` sliding by `margin-inline-start` (`state-checked:ms-4`); `sm` and `lg` scale by one step (changed in review r2: an `rtl:`/`:dir()` flip breaks in production builds) | new tokens; `translate-x` with a direction flip | Numeric spacing is allowed for layout inside an item. The logical margin follows the element's own direction, so the thumb ends at the inline end. |

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** Spec, Props, Events and Keyboard: `v-model` toggles on click, on a click on its text, on `Space` and on `Enter`; `role="switch"` with `aria-checked`.
- **AC-002** Spec, Props: attributes reach the control and `class` the wrapper; each size renders its track and text classes; the thumb sits at the inline end when on, mirrored in RTL.
- **AC-003** Spec, States and Accessibility: `disabled` blocks toggling, dims the track and mutes the text; on fills the track with `primary`; the focus ring shows on keyboard focus.
- **AC-004** Spec, Props: with `name`, the form gets `value` while on and nothing while off; `required` blocks submit while off; the hidden input lies under the track, hands focus to the switch, and fires `input`/`change` on a user's toggle only.
- **AC-005** Definition of done 3: axe reports no violations in the demo (every state in the spec's States), light and dark.
- **AC-006** Definition of done 1, 4–6 and the docs page: `pnpm check:item switch` passes and the docs site builds a `switch` page whose tables match the types; `checkbox` and `radio-group` still pass on the shared helper.

Not bugs: versions stay 0.1.0 before the first publish; a shared composable (ticket).
