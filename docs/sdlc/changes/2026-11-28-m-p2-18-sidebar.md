# P2-18 Sidebar

## Why

**Request (verbatim):** P2-18 sidebar component (tasks/phase-2.md, Tier 2 components, Recipe C). Unattended run: take recommended options, record delegated decisions, open a PR per task, ask before merging to public main.

Task [P2-18](../../../tasks/phase-2.md#tier-2-components), Recipe C: `sidebar`. Catalog row: Reka Collapsible, parts Header, Content, Group, Item, Footer, Trigger, "`v-model:collapsed`. Becomes a drawer below 768 px." The spec is [registry/ui/sidebar/sidebar.md](../../../registry/ui/sidebar/sidebar.md). It is the navigation region of the `app-shell` layout (P2-20), and [layout-rules.md](../../../docs/layout-rules.md#responsive) says the sidebar becomes a drawer below 768 px.

## Decisions

| Decision | Ruled out | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `Sidebar` is a Reka `CollapsibleRoot` rendered as a `nav` named by `navLabel` (default `Navigation`); `collapsed` is the inverse of the collapsible's `open`, so `data-state="closed"` means collapsed and parts style off `group-state-closed/sidebar` | A plain `div`; a `CollapsibleContent` that hides the items | The catalog names Collapsible; a collapsed sidebar narrows to `sidebar-width-collapsed` and keeps its icons, so nothing is hidden and no content part is needed. `navLabel` follows `breadcrumb`. |
| Below 768 px (`md`) the `nav` is hidden by CSS and the same slot renders inside a `drawer` (`side="start"`, `w-sidebar-width`), opened by a second model, `v-model:open`; the drawer's title is `navLabel`, visually hidden, and its close button is named by `closeLabel` | Reading `matchMedia` at setup; a provider part that wraps the page | CSS keeps it SSR-safe with no hydration mismatch; the catalog has no provider part, and the `app-shell` topbar owns the menu button. |
| The drawer closes when an item in it is clicked and when the window grows to 768 px or wider (a `ResizeObserver` on the hidden `nav`, after mount) | Leaving the drawer and its overlay open over the desktop layout | A modal drawer left open on a wide window keeps the page inert behind an overlay. Watching the `nav` avoids a breakpoint literal in code. |
| `SidebarTrigger` must sit inside `Sidebar` (header or footer); it toggles `collapsed`, carries `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` (the `nav`'s id), is named by `toggleLabel` (default `Toggle sidebar`) and is not rendered in the drawer copy | A trigger that works anywhere on the page | Reka's trigger reads the root's context; Reka's own `aria-controls` names a content id that never exists here, so the `nav`'s id replaces it. The page's menu button below 768 px sets `open`. |
| `SidebarGroup` takes a `heading` prop and renders a `role="group"` named by it, with a list of items; `SidebarItem` is an `li` around a `Primitive` (`as` default `a`, `asChild`, `href`, `active` for `aria-current="page"`) with a `leading` slot for its icon | A GroupLabel part; items without a list | `combobox`'s `heading` is the precedent; `breadcrumb`'s link keeps `href` off an `asChild` child. |
| Collapsed, the group heading and item text are visually hidden (`sr-only`) but stay the accessible names; the trigger's chevron points to the start when expanded and to the end when collapsed, from Reka's `useDirection` | A tooltip on each collapsed item; a CSS direction selector | Tooltip needs a provider at the root and isn't in the catalog row; `:dir()` breaks in production builds (registry gotchas). |
| Uses the icons `icon` 0.2.0 already has (`chevron-left`, `chevron-right`) | A new panel icon | `add` can't upgrade an installed `icon` (registry gotchas). |

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** Spec, Accessibility and Props (`navLabel`, `heading`, `href`, `active`, `as`, `asChild`, `class`): the sidebar is a `navigation` landmark named `Navigation` by default or by `navLabel`; a group is a `group` named by its heading; items are links to their `href`, the `active` one with `aria-current="page"` and the others without; an `asChild` item keeps its child's own `href`; `as="button"` renders a button; `class` and attributes land on the `nav`.
- **AC-002** Spec, Props (`collapsed`, `toggleLabel`) and Keyboard: expanded, the `nav` is `sidebar-width` wide and the trigger, named `Toggle sidebar` or by `toggleLabel`, has `aria-expanded="true"` and `aria-controls` naming the `nav`; clicking it, Enter or Space toggles `collapsed` (emitting `update:collapsed`), narrows the `nav` to `sidebar-width-collapsed` and sets `aria-expanded="false"`; `collapsed: true` renders collapsed.
- **AC-003** Spec, States (collapsed): collapsed, item text and group headings are visually hidden but items keep their names and groups their names; the leading icons stay visible inside the `nav`.
- **AC-004** Spec, States (below 768 px) and Props (`open`, `closeLabel`): at 375 px the `nav` is not displayed; `open: true` shows a modal `dialog` named by `navLabel` against the start edge (the right edge in right-to-left text), `sidebar-width` wide, with the items labelled even when `collapsed` and no trigger; its close button (`Close` or `closeLabel`), Escape and a click on an item close it, emitting `update:open` with `false`.
- **AC-005** Spec, States (resize): a drawer open at 375 px closes when the window grows to 1024 px, and `open: true` on a wide window closes at mount; neither leaves a dialog behind.
- **AC-006** Spec, States (long content): with more items than fit, only `SidebarContent` scrolls, the header and footer stay inside the `nav`, tabbing to the last item scrolls it into view, and every item sits far enough inside the scrolling content that its focus ring isn't clipped.
- **AC-007** Definition of done 3: axe reports no violations in every demo state (expanded and collapsed), light and dark, and at 375 px with the drawer open.
- **AC-008** Definition of done 1, 4–6 and the docs page: the registry build includes `sidebar`, and the docs site builds its page and demo.

## Must never break

- Every other item, its baselines and its docs page stay as they are; `drawer`, `button` and `icon` are not changed.
- No token value or name changes, and `@heart-ui/theme` is not changed.
- Built CSS never uses `:lang(` (logical properties only, checked by `tests/playgrounds.test.ts`).
- No `window`, `document` or `matchMedia` at setup: the demo renders under Nuxt SSR with no hydration warning.

## Out of scope

- A tooltip on collapsed items, nested items (sub-menus), a rail to drag the width, and remembering `collapsed` across visits: not in the catalog row.
- The topbar and its menu button: the `app-shell` layout (P2-20).

## Review

- **What fired, and who chose it:** code and security, chosen from the trigger list without asking (low risk). Code fired because `## Public Surface` is not `- none` (the `Sidebar*` exports). Security fired because `.changeset/sidebar.md` matches `areas.sensitive` (`.changeset/**`); it ran after the code rounds, once the agent noticed the trigger. UI didn't fire: config has no `areas.ui`.
- **Verdicts:**
  - Security r1 (`sdlc:code-reviewer` on opus) → `security-r1.md` → `VERDICT: PASS`. Caller text reaches the DOM only through escaped bindings; `href` never reaches an `asChild` child or a button; the pin matches the other items; the changeset is a minor bump.
  - Dependency audit r1 (`pnpm audit --prod`) → `dependency-audit-r1.md` → `VERDICT: PASS`. The same two pre-existing moderates (`glob`, `minimatch`, in the `apps/playground-nuxt` build chain), rated low.
  - Code r1 (built-in `code-review high`) → `code-reviewer-r1.md` → `VERDICT: FAIL — CR-001, CR-002, CR-004, CR-006, CR-007, CR-008`. High: CR-001 (a drawer opened on a wide window stayed open over the nav), CR-007 (`px-3` on a control). Bugs: lost focus after a resize close, modified clicks closing the drawer, an `a` without `href`, a stale `aria-controls`. CR-003 and CR-005 became spec notes.
  - Code r2 → `code-reviewer-r2.md` → `VERDICT: FAIL — CR2-001, CR2-002`. High: item attributes landed on the `li`; the focus fallback picked the header's trigger. Bugs: focus moved on page load, the trigger bound to a nested collapsible, an empty `id`, footer text wrapping in the rail. **The user chose one more fix round** at the cap.
  - Code r3 → `code-reviewer-r3.md` → `VERDICT: FAIL — CR3-001, CR3-002`. High: a `RouterLink` item never closed the drawer (it cancels every click), and an unset `aria-current` replaced the link's own. **The user chose one more fix round.**
  - Code r4 → `code-reviewer-r4.md` → `VERDICT: FAIL — CR4-001, CR4-002`. High: collapsed `sr-only` text escaped the scroller and grew the page; the round-3 display fix put library classes after the caller's `class`. **The user chose one more fix round.**
  - Code r5 → `code-reviewer-r5.md` → `VERDICT: PASS` (no high). Its bugs were fixed test first without another round: CR5-001 (the drawer's exit animation played over a wide window), CR5-002 (confirmed by a test with compiled slots: a changed `id` left `aria-controls` stale), CR5-004 (the focus fallback without a current item).
- **Fixed:** every finding rated a bug, each with a failing test first, in `registry/ui/sidebar/Sidebar.test.ts` (42 tests). The drawer now opens only on a narrow window (its `open` is gated on a measured flag), so it never shows on a wide one; the trigger toggles through the sidebar's own context, not Reka's nearest collapsible; `SidebarItem` forwards attributes to its link or button, renders a `button` without an `href`, binds `aria-current` only when `active`, and closes the drawer on any plain primary click; item and group are `relative`, so collapsed hidden text stays inside the scroller; the `nav` hides with `max-md:hidden` and merges `class` last; header and footer clip to the rail.
- **Tickets:**
  - CR4-004: `SidebarTrigger` throws outside a `Sidebar`, as Reka's parts do outside their root; a softer failure would need a convention.
  - CR4-005: `transition-all` also fades the nav's colours on a theme switch; a width-only transition needs a theme utility (`transition-[width]` is an arbitrary value).
  - CR5-005: the chevron reads direction from Reka's `ConfigProvider` only, as `breadcrumb` and `pagination` do; an RTL page without it shows it mirrored.
  - CR5-003, CR5-006: an explicit `as="a"` needs an `href` (spec note); `px-0` in the rail is zero, not a length.
- **Drift and manual checks:** see the state file's `## Deviations`: test-lock changes (each re-run red at Base), the internal `context.ts` exports, the trigger no longer on Reka's `CollapsibleTrigger` (the root still is a `CollapsibleRoot`), spec notes, and a `docs/conventions.md` reference row. There are no manual ACs. `pnpm check:item sidebar` passed after the last fix. The 12 visual baselines need human approval.
