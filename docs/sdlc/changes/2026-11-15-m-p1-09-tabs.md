## Decisions

| Decision | Ruled out | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `variant` and `orientation` are `Tabs` props and reach the list and triggers by `provide`/`inject`, as `radio-group`'s `size` does | a prop on each part | One tab set, one look. |
| The active trigger is styled with Tailwind's `aria-selected:` (Reka sets `aria-selected="true"` on it) | a `state-active` theme variant | No theme change needed. |
| `line`: the list has a `border` rule on its block end (inline end when vertical) and the active trigger a `primary` bar over it; `pill`: the list is a `muted` track with `p-1` and the active trigger a `background` chip with `shadow-sm` | — | The two catalog variants. |
| Vertical tabs lay the list out as a column beside the panels (`Tabs` becomes a row) | — | The common vertical layout. |
| Panels keep Reka's `tabindex="0"` and get a focus ring | — | A panel without focusable content must be reachable. |

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** Spec, Props and Events: a click on a trigger activates it and shows its panel; `v-model` follows; a disabled trigger can't be activated.
- **AC-002** Spec, Keyboard: arrows move and activate along the orientation (right/left horizontal, down/up vertical), wrapping; `Home`/`End`; `Tab` goes from the active tab into its panel.
- **AC-003** Spec, Props and Tokens: each variant styles the list and the active trigger as described; vertical lays the list out as a column; `class` merges last on each part.
- **AC-004** Spec, Accessibility: `tablist` with `aria-orientation` and the list's name, `tab` with `aria-selected` and `aria-controls`, `tabpanel` labelled by its tab.
- **AC-005** Definition of done 3: axe reports no violations in the demo (both variants, both orientations, a disabled tab), light and dark.
- **AC-006** Definition of done 1, 4–6 and the docs page: `pnpm check:item tabs` passes and the docs site builds a `tabs` page whose tables match the types.
