# P2-15 Drawer

## Why

**Request (verbatim):** P2-15 drawer component (tasks/phase-2.md), unattended per memory: decide recommended options, PR per task, ask before merge

Task [P2-15](../../../tasks/phase-2.md#tier-2-components), Recipe C: `drawer`. Catalog row: Reka Dialog, parts Trigger, Content, Header, Title, Description, Footer, Close, "`side`: start, end, bottom. Content `size`." The spec is [registry/ui/drawer/drawer.md](../../../registry/ui/drawer/drawer.md). Layout rules call for a side drawer for quick view or edit, and `sidebar` (P2-18) becomes a drawer below 768 px.

## Decisions

| Decision | Ruled out | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `side` and `size` are props of `DrawerContent`, `side` defaulting to `end` | `side` on the root | The catalog puts `size` on the content, and `dialog` (the reference) keeps every look prop there; shadcn's sheet does the same. |
| `size` caps width on a side drawer (24, 32, 48 rem, as `dialog`) and height on a bottom drawer (a third, a half, three quarters of the window) | One meaning for both; ignoring `size` on the bottom | A bottom drawer is already full width; its height is what a caller tunes. |
| A side drawer slides by animating `inset-inline-start` or `-end` from -100% on the relatively positioned panel inside a layer that shrinks to the panel's width; the bottom drawer animates `translate` | `translate-x` keyframes; theme animations | Translate is physical, so `start` would slide the wrong way in right-to-left text, and `:dir()` and `rtl:` are ruled out (registry gotchas). The keyframes sit in a private `@layer heart-drawer` in `DrawerContent.vue`, as `accordion` does, so a project with the published `@heart-ui/theme` needs no theme upgrade. |
| Copy `dialog`'s parts and behaviour (description count, `close` after the exit, focus on a scrolling body, `dismissible`, `showClose`, `closeLabel`) into the drawer's own files | Depending on the `dialog` item and re-exporting its parts | Items are copied source; a drawer that re-styles dialog parts couples two items' versions. |
| No swipe to dismiss and no drag handle | vaul-style gestures | Not in the catalog row; Reka Dialog has none. |

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** Spec, Accessibility and Events: the trigger opens a `dialog` named by its title and described by its description; `DrawerClose`, Escape and a click on the overlay close it, updating `v-model:open`; `close` fires once after the panel is gone and not on a teardown while open; without a description it has no `aria-describedby` and logs no warning; without a title it has no name, warns, and axe reports `aria-dialog-name`.
- **AC-002** Spec, Props (`dismissible`, `showClose`, `closeLabel`): `dismissible: false` keeps it open on Escape and an outside click; the close button is named `Close` by default or by `closeLabel`, closes the drawer, and `showClose: false` removes it and the header's room for it.
- **AC-003** Spec, Keyboard and Accessibility: focus moves in on open, cycles with Tab and Shift+Tab, and returns to the trigger on close; a modal drawer hides the page from assistive tech and locks its scroll; `modal: false` leaves the page alone.
- **AC-004** Spec, Props (`side`): by default the panel is the full window height against the end edge; `start` puts it against the start edge and `bottom` against the bottom edge at full width; in right-to-left text `start` is the right edge and `end` the left.
- **AC-005** Spec, Tokens and States (slide): while opening, a side panel moves in from beyond its own edge (left of the window for `start` in left-to-right text, right of it in right-to-left text) and a bottom panel from below the window; it ends flush with its edge.
- **AC-006** Spec, Props (`size`): side drawers have a max width of 24, 32 and 48 rem for `sm`, `md` (default) and `lg`; bottom drawers a max height of a third, a half and three quarters of the window; `class` merges last on the panel, and `class="w-sidebar-width"` sets its width; at 375 px a side drawer fills the window width.
- **AC-007** Spec, States (long content): long content scrolls inside the panel, which stays within the window, the close button stays in its corner, the footer sits at the bottom of a short side drawer, and opening by keyboard focuses the panel at the top of a scrolling body.
- **AC-008** Definition of done 3: axe reports no violations in every demo state, light and dark, and at 375 px.
- **AC-009** Definition of done 1, 4–6 and the docs page: the registry build includes `drawer`, and the docs site builds its page and demo, marked as an overlay preview.

## Must never break

- Every other item, its baselines and its docs page stay as they are; `dialog` is not changed.
- No token value or name changes, and `@heart-ui/theme` is not changed.
- Built CSS never uses `:lang(` (logical properties only, checked by `tests/playgrounds.test.ts`).
- No `window` or `document` at setup: the demo renders under Nuxt SSR with no hydration warning.

## Out of scope

- Swipe to dismiss, a drag handle, snap points and a top side: not in the catalog row.
- The `sidebar` below 768 px (P2-18) builds on this drawer.
